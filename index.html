Areas of Support

What Can We Help With?

We assist customers with understanding and preparing for a wide range of electric service situations.

New Service Concerns

Assistance with understanding meter application requirements, clearances, and preparation steps.

Billing Questions

Guidance on understanding statement breakdowns, estimated consumption, and billing inquiries.

Power Interruptions

Information on how to report localized outages, identify feeder issues, and check restoration channels.

Meter Concerns

Questions regarding meter location, meter post safety, calibration queries, and reading verification.

Service Requests

Assistance with organizing requests for reconnection, account name changes, or meter relocation.

General Inquiries

Help with general questions, customer service contacts, and understanding Philippine utility procedures.

Notice: For official utility actions, account changes, billing transactions, and service requests, customers may need to coordinate directly with the appropriate electric utility provider.
