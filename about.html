About DBB Industrial Services

DBB Industrial Services was registered with the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) since December of 1997. It is a single proprietorship form of business owned and managed by Engr. Dorotheo B. Company Overview

Core scope and operational profile

DBB Industrial Services specializes in electrical works and provides manpower, installation, maintenance, industrial electrical, and infrastructure-related services.

Operational Footprint: Operating actively across Metro Cebu, Cebu Province, and adjacent Visayan territories, coordinating turnkey electrical installations, transmission line stringing, transformer banking, and institutional utility services.
